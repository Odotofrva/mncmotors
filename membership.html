COMMON QUESTIONS

GOOD TO KNOW.

Is membership open now?

You can submit a community interest request now. Submitting a request does not automatically grant membership or access to future benefits.

Does membership cost anything?

Membership pricing, if any, has not been announced. The Automotive Fundamentals Manual is free.

Can I use the card at automotive businesses?

Not yet. Participating-location passes and partner benefits are concepts being explored, not currently redeemable offers.

Do I need to own an import car?

No. The community welcomes aspiring technicians, learners, designers, makers, and enthusiasts at different stages of their automotive journey.

How do I apply?

Select Request to Join to reach the community interest form on the main website. Applications can be processed once the site's registration service is configured.
